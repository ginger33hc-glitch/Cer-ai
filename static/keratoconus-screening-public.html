Keratoconus screening

CER-AI supports ophthalmologists reviewing manifest refraction and five Pentacam images for keratoconus screening. Results: negative, suspicious or positive.

Enter the patient’s name, age and manifest refraction, then upload five Pentacam images: right and left Four Maps Refractive, right and left Belin/Ambrosio Display, and one Show 2 Exams Topometric report.

Unreadable measurements are requested from the surgeon. Screening uses the entered patient name and age; no LASIK, PRK or SMILE procedure selection is required.

Results are shown for each eye as negative, suspicious or positive. A PDF report can be opened, downloaded and shared after screening is completed.

Screening is clinical decision support. A positive result is not a confirmed keratoconus diagnosis; a negative result does not exclude disease. An ophthalmologist must interpret the findings.
