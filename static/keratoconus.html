Keratoconus Screening

Screening decision support. Positive does not mean a confirmed diagnosis; negative does not exclude keratoconus. An ophthalmologist must review the findings.

Start in 3 steps

  1. Upload 5 Pentacam images together
    • 4 Maps Refractive: OD + OS (2 images)
    • Belin/Ambrósio Display (BAD-D): OD + OS (2 images)
    • Show 2 Exams Topometric: both eyes (1 image)
  2. Enter patient name, age and manifest refraction for both eyes.
  3. Click Run screening; complete any unreadable values, then review or download the report.
